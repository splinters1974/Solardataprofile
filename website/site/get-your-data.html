How to get your half-hourly data

Most organisations already have the data; it just isn't on the bill. Here is where to find it and what to ask for.

Ask your electricity supplier for half-hourly consumption data for each meter, for the last 12 months or more, as a spreadsheet. Many suppliers also let you download it from their online account.

Who has it

If your meter records half-hourly readings, your supplier receives them and can send them to you. Larger business sites have had half-hourly meters for years, and more sites are moving to half-hourly settlement, so it's worth asking even for smaller premises.

Other places to look:

  • Your supplier's online portal: often under "consumption", "usage" or "data download".
  • Your energy broker or energy manager, if you have one.
  • A building management or monitoring system that exports meter data.
  • Smart meter data through an app or service that offers half-hourly downloads.

What to ask for

Something like this works:

Example request
Please send the half-hourly consumption data (kWh) for meter [MPAN], from [date] to [date], as an Excel or CSV file with one row per day and 48 half-hourly columns.
  • One file per meter. Name each file after the site; the name becomes the site name in the analyser.
  • At least 12 months, so annual figures and seasonal patterns are reliable. Up to three years is useful for trends.
  • kWh per half hour is ideal. kW files also work; the analyser tells you which it found.

Your meter's MPAN (the 13-digit supply number) is on your electricity bill.
