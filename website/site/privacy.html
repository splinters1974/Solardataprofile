Privacy

Privacy notice

Short version: your meter data never leaves your computer. If you contact us, we use your details only to reply.

The analyser

The analyser runs entirely in your web browser. Meter files you load are read on your computer. They are not uploaded, and we never see them. The same applies to everything you enter (opening hours, rates, notes) and everything you download (PDFs, spreadsheets, project files and customer dashboards).

The analyser stores nothing in your browser between visits. Save a project file if you want to come back to your work.

Enquiries

If you send the contact form, we receive the details you enter: your name, email, organisation, phone, sector, number of sites and message. We use them only to reply to your enquiry and any follow-up you ask for. We don't sell them or add you to a mailing list.

The form is processed by our website host, Netlify, which stores submissions on our behalf. [Retention period and data controller details to be confirmed.]

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no advertising or tracking scripts. Fonts are served from this site, not a third party.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, by contacting us. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
