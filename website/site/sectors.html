Sectors

What to look for in your sector

Every building type has its own normal. These are the patterns worth checking first.

Schools and colleges

Holiday weeks and weekends should look almost flat. Look for heating, ventilation and IT left on through the holidays, and for evening lettings that run the whole building for one room.

Offices and council buildings

Use should fall sharply after hours. Watch for early starts well before staff arrive, air handling running all night, and floors lit for a cleaner or one late worker.

Leisure centres and pools

Long opening hours and pools that run around the clock make base load high. The question is what the floor is made of: pool plant, ventilation and dehumidifiers are often set once and left.

Healthcare

Many sites are genuinely 24/7, so the useful comparisons are day against night and weekday against weekend, and how far peaks sit from supply capacity before new electric heating goes in.

Industrial and warehouses

Shift patterns make the calendar important. Look at shutdown periods, compressed air and refrigeration overnight, and peaks against capacity before adding EV charging or electrification.

Retail and hospitality

Trading hours are fixed, so out-of-hours use is easy to see. Check refrigeration, signage, kitchen extraction and heating schedules that do not follow closing time.

These are general starting points, not rules. The analyser compares each site with its own calendar, so set the opening hours first.
