Questions and answers

If your question isn't here, ask us.

Using the analyser

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no charge, no account and no limit on the number of sites. If you want help acting on the results, you can contact us, but you don't have to.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in Chrome or Edge on a recent computer. It isn't designed for phones; the charts need a bigger screen.

How many sites can I load?

As many as your computer will hold. One file per meter; each becomes a site, and the analyser ranks them so you know where to start.

Can I save my work?

Yes. Save a project file to your computer and open it again later. It holds the readings and every setting, so treat it like the original data.

Can I share results with a client or colleague?

Download the site or portfolio PDF, export to Excel, or make a customer dashboard: a locked, password-protected copy of the analyser holding just their sites.

Your data

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The analysis runs entirely in your browser. Meter files are read on your computer and nothing is sent to us or anyone else. You can disconnect from the internet once the page has loaded and keep working.

What data do I need?

Half-hourly electricity readings for each meter, ideally 12 months or more. Here is how to get them.

What if my file has gaps or errors?

The analyser finds them before it shows any figure. Missing readings are left out, never counted as zero; zero days and clock-change days are flagged for you to decide on.

The results

Are the costs savings?

No. They are what a pattern costs now at the rates you entered. A saving depends on what you change. The savings scenarios are what-ifs, clearly labelled.

How accurate is it?

As accurate as the data and the opening hours you give it. Every finding shows its confidence and the evidence behind it, and every figure says whether it was measured or estimated.

Can it tell me which equipment is wasting energy?

No. Whole-site data shows when energy is used, not by what. The findings tell you where and when to look; a site visit finds the cause.
